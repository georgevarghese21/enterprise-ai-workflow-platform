# NovaTech Remote Work & Time Off Policy

## Purpose

This policy covers remote work arrangements and paid time off (PTO)
requests for NovaTech employees.

## Remote work

NovaTech operates a hybrid model: employees are expected in a NovaTech
office at least 2 days per week unless they hold a fully-remote role
designation. Requests to work remotely for a short period (up to 10
consecutive business days, e.g. working from a family member's home) are
auto-approved as long as the employee remains in the same country and
notifies their manager in advance; no formal approval step is required.

## Extended or international remote work

Remote work requests longer than 10 consecutive business days, or any
remote work from outside the employee's home country, require manager
approval and a review by HR for tax and legal compliance reasons. These
requests should be submitted at least 3 weeks in advance.

## Paid time off (PTO)

Full-time employees accrue PTO according to their tenure band, tracked in
the HR system. PTO requests of 5 consecutive business days or fewer are
auto-approved as long as the employee has sufficient accrued balance;
NovaTech does not require manager approval for standard PTO within
balance, only advance notice of at least 3 business days.

## Extended leave

PTO requests longer than 5 consecutive business days, or any leave that
would take an employee's accrued balance negative, require manager
approval. Leave related to a qualifying life event (parental leave,
medical leave) follows NovaTech's separate Leave of Absence process
administered by HR rather than the standard PTO flow.

## Contractor time off

Contractors do not accrue NovaTech PTO; time off for contractors is
governed by their individual contract terms and does not go through the
employee PTO approval flow.

## Blackout periods

HR may designate short blackout periods (e.g. around a major product
launch) during which extended PTO requests from a given department require
additional Head-of-department approval even if within the 5-day
auto-approval threshold.
