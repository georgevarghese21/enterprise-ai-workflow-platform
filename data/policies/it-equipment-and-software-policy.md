# NovaTech IT Equipment & Software Policy

## Purpose

This policy covers how employees request laptops, peripherals, and
software licenses, and the rules IT support follows when handling those
requests.

## Standard equipment requests

New full-time employees are issued a standard laptop (MacBook Pro or
equivalent ThinkPad, employee's choice) and a monitor during onboarding at
no approval step required beyond HR's onboarding checklist. Replacement
laptops due to hardware failure are auto-approved by IT once a ticket is
filed; no manager approval is needed for a like-for-like replacement.

## Non-standard equipment

Requests for equipment beyond the standard kit — a second monitor, a
higher-spec laptop, specialized hardware such as GPUs for local ML
development — require manager approval before IT will fulfill the
request. Requests over $2,000 additionally require Finance approval.

## Software licenses

Software is split into two categories:

- **Pre-approved software list**: Tools already vetted by Security and IT
  (common developer tools, standard productivity software) can be
  installed or licensed by any employee without approval, subject to
  license seat availability.
- **New software requests**: Any tool not on the pre-approved list requires
  Security review before purchase or installation, because Security needs
  to assess data handling and vendor risk. Security review typically takes
  3-5 business days.

## Contractor equipment

Contractors are not issued NovaTech-owned laptops by default; they are
expected to use their own hardware unless their contract explicitly states
otherwise. When a contract does provide for NovaTech equipment, the same
non-standard equipment approval rules apply, and the equipment must be
returned within 5 business days of contract end.

## IT support tickets

All equipment and software requests are tracked as IT tickets. Standard
requests are typically fulfilled within 2 business days; requests requiring
approval are fulfilled within 2 business days of approval being recorded.
Employees should file a ticket rather than requesting equipment directly
from an IT staff member, so that there is a record for asset tracking.

## Offboarding

All NovaTech-owned equipment must be returned on or before an employee's
last day. IT revokes software license access as part of the standard
offboarding checklist, independent of equipment return.
