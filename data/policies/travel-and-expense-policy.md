# NovaTech Travel & Expense Policy

## Purpose

This policy governs business travel booking and expense reimbursement for
NovaTech employees.

## Booking business travel

Domestic flights and hotels under $1,500 total may be booked directly by
the employee through the company travel portal without prior approval;
the trip is auto-approved as long as it is tied to a valid business
purpose (client visit, conference, team offsite, interview support).
International travel, or any single trip over $1,500, requires manager
approval before booking.

## Airfare and hotel class

Employees should book economy class for flights under 6 hours and may book
premium economy for flights over 6 hours. Business class requires VP-level
approval regardless of trip cost. Hotel bookings should target a nightly
rate at or below the company's per-city guideline; exceeding the guideline
by more than 20% requires manager approval even if the trip itself was
auto-approved.

## Expense reimbursement

Expenses under $100 per item (meals, local transport, incidentals) are
auto-approved for reimbursement as long as a receipt is attached. Expenses
between $100 and $1,000 require manager approval. Expenses over $1,000
require both manager and Finance approval.

## Per diem

Employees traveling domestically receive a $75/day meal per diem in lieu
of itemized meal receipts; itemizing meals instead of taking the per diem
is allowed but requires receipts for every meal claimed. International per
diem rates vary by destination and are published separately by Finance.

## Non-reimbursable expenses

The following are never reimbursable regardless of amount or approval:
personal entertainment, alcohol purchased outside of a client-facing team
dinner, traffic or parking fines, and upgrades to first/business class
booked without the required VP approval.

## Corporate card misuse

Any expense flagged as clearly personal (non-business) use of a corporate
card is escalated directly to Finance and HR regardless of dollar amount,
bypassing the normal approval thresholds above.
