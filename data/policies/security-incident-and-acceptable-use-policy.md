# NovaTech Security Incident & Acceptable Use Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines acceptable use of NovaTech systems and how security
incidents must be reported and handled.

## Acceptable use

NovaTech-owned accounts, devices, and cloud resources are to be used for
business purposes. Incidental personal use (checking personal email,
browsing during a break) is acceptable and does not require reporting.
Installing unapproved software, disabling endpoint security agents, or
sharing account credentials with another person are all violations of this
policy regardless of intent.

## Reporting a security incident

Any suspected security incident — a phishing email that was clicked, a
lost or stolen device, unauthorized access to a system, unusual account
activity — must be reported to Security immediately, and always within 24
hours of discovery. Reporting is never penalized, including when the
employee themselves caused the incident (e.g. clicking a phishing link);
NovaTech's policy is to encourage fast reporting over blame.

## Incident severity and response

Security triages every reported incident into a severity level:

- **Low severity** (e.g. a suspicious email that was not clicked): Logged
  by Security, no immediate action required from the reporting employee.
- **Medium severity** (e.g. a phishing link was clicked but no credentials
  entered): Security may require a password reset and device scan.
- **High severity** (e.g. credentials were entered on a phishing site, a
  device was lost with unencrypted data, unauthorized production access
  occurred): Security leads an active incident response; affected access
  is revoked immediately pending investigation, and the employee's manager
  is notified.

## Data handling

Customer data, regardless of sensitivity tier, may never be copied to a
personal device, personal cloud storage, or a personal email account.
Exporting HIGH sensitivity data for any reason requires a documented
business justification and Security sign-off in advance, not after the
fact.

## Device security

All NovaTech-owned devices must have full-disk encryption and the
standard endpoint security agent enabled at all times. Disabling the
endpoint agent, even temporarily for troubleshooting, requires an active
IT support ticket and must be re-enabled within the same business day.

## Consequences

Repeated or willful violations of this policy (as opposed to a good-faith
mistake that was promptly reported) are handled jointly by Security and HR
and may affect continued access to NovaTech systems.
